# NexRx Power Architecture

The NexRx receiver is powered primarily from the USB VBUS supply (5V at 3A expected). This provides up to 15W of power, which is significantly more than required for the triple-QSD architecture and supporting digital logic.

## Power Rail Summary

| Rail | Voltage | Capacity | Control | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VBUS** | 5.0V | 3.0A | Always On | Primary input power from USB-C. |
| **+3.3V** | 3.3V | 3.0A | Always On | Main logic supply (MCU, FPGA IO). Generated by U1201 (LMR51430). |
| **+6V** | 6.0V | 300mA* | `Ctrl.enable6V` | High-voltage bias for RF chains. Generated by U1202 (TPS6123). |
| **+5VA** | 5.0V | 300mA | `Ctrl.enable5VA` | Analog supply for AK5578 32-bit ADCs. Generated by U1203 (TPS7A92050). |
| **+3.3VA** | 3.3V | 300mA | `Ctrl.enable3VA` | Clean analog supply for PGA and buffers. Generated by U1205 (TPS7A2033). |
| **+1.2V** | 1.2V | 1.0A | `Ctrl.enableFPGAVCORE` | FPGA Core supply. Generated by U1207 (TPS74612). |
| **+3.3V-ON** | 3.3V | 2.0A | `Ctrl.enable3V3` | Switched logic supply for peripherals. Controlled by U1204 (TPS22918). |

## Design Verification Notes

1.  **Input Protection:** The VBUS input includes ESD protection (D1201, D1203) and a TVS diode (D1202) to clamp surges.
2.  **Conversion Efficiency:** The main 3.3V rail uses a synchronous buck converter (LMR51430) for high efficiency, minimizing heat dissipation within the enclosure.
3.  **Analog Integrity:** Sensitive analog rails (+5VA, +3.3VA) utilize high-PSRR LDOs (TPS7A series) to ensure the high dynamic range of the ADCs is not compromised by switching noise.
4.  **Sequencing:** All critical sub-systems are gated by STM32 GPIOs (`enable` signals), allowing the MCU to manage power-up sequencing and reduce inrush current.
5.  **Thermal Management:** The choice of 3A capacity for the primary 3.3V rail provides significant headroom, ensuring the regulator runs cool even during peak FPGA processing.
